# Privacy Policy — Jobber Keyboard Shortcuts

**Last updated: 2026-06-14**

Jobber Keyboard Shortcuts does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any
personal or usage data.

- It runs only on `https://secure.getjobber.com/*`.
- It listens for keyboard shortcuts and clicks the corresponding buttons or
  links that already exist on the Jobber page.
- It makes **no** network requests, uses **no** cookies or storage, and sends
  **nothing** to the author or any third party.

Because the extension processes no data, there is nothing to delete or request.

Questions? Open an issue at
https://github.com/bendelaney/jobber-keyboard-shortcuts
